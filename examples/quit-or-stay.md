# Golden Case 7 — Strategy Synthesis (Quit Now vs Endure)

## Scenario

User: "我非常不满意现在的工作。我是今天直接辞职，还是继续忍半年？"

Additional context (revealed when asked or inferred):
- Has 6 months of savings
- No new offer yet
- Industry job search typically takes 2-4 months
- Current job is unpleasant but no immediate safety risk
- No debt, no dependents

---

## What This Case Validates

### 1. Strategy Synthesis

The user presents a binary choice: quit NOW vs endure 6 months.

Both options have clear flaws:
- **Quit now:** Financial runway is tight (6 months savings, 2-4 month search = cutting it close). No leverage in negotiation.
- **Endure 6 months:** Continued dissatisfaction, potential burnout, arbitrary deadline.

A good Skill should identify a **third path** that neither option captures:

> **Start job searching now, while still employed. Set a concrete exit trigger (e.g., "once I have an offer"). This gives you leverage, preserves runway, and replaces the arbitrary 6-month deadline with an event-driven one.**

Or a contingent strategy:
> "If you can secure interviews within the next 2-4 weeks while working, do that. If the current job is so draining that you can't job-search effectively, then consider a short leave or reduced-effort mode rather than full resignation."

### 2. Complexity Gate

This is MEDIUM-HIGH:
- Significant stakes (income, career)
- Partially reversible (can find new job)
- No immediate danger
- Path dependency (unemployment gap)

### 3. Contingent Strategy

Rather than a binary choice, the answer should be conditional:
- The real decision is not "quit vs endure" — it's about **when and under what conditions** to leave
- The exit should be tied to a concrete event (offer received), not an arbitrary timeline (6 months)

### 4. Event Chain

Should simulate forward:
```
Quit now → 2-4 month search → potentially stressful final months of savings → may accept suboptimal offer under pressure
Start searching while employed → 2-4 months → leave with offer in hand → financial continuity → better negotiation position
```

### 5. Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Simply comparing "quit now pros/cons" vs "endure pros/cons"
- ❌ Recommending endurance without acknowledging the real cost of burnout
- ❌ Recommending immediate quit without noting financial runway risk
- ❌ Not recognizing that the binary framing is itself the problem

---

## Minimum Acceptable Behavior

1. Identifies that the binary framing (quit now vs endure 6 months) is suboptimal
2. Proposes at least one third strategy (search while employed, event-driven exit)
3. Simulates forward consequences for the proposed path
4. Does not default to "stay" out of conservatism
5. Response is actionable: concrete next step, not just analysis
